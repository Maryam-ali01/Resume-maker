A resume builder that stays out of your way.

Inkfolio was put together for one job: help you write a resume you'd actually be proud to send, without fighting a bloated editor to do it.

01

Everything runs in your browser

Your details are saved to this browser only, as you type, so refreshing the page never costs you your work.

02

Print or save as PDF

The builder's Export button opens your browser's print dialog with a layout tuned for a clean single PDF page.

03

No accounts, no lock-in

No sign-up, no watermark, no premium templates hidden behind a paywall — pick a layout and start typing.

FAQ

A few things people ask.

Can I change the accent colour after choosing a layout?

Yes — the colour swatches and font selector in the builder's Customise panel apply live to whichever layout is active.

Does my photo get uploaded anywhere?

No — the image is read directly in your browser and drawn into the preview; it never leaves your device.

Can I switch layouts without losing what I typed?

Yes — your entries (experience, education, skills, photo) carry over automatically when you switch templates.
